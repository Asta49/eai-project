Routine diagnostic blood monitoring enables objective quantification of metabolic health, endocrine status, and nutritional adequacy in PCOS.

### Baseline Panel (Recommended at diagnosis or onboarding):
1. **Metabolic:** Fasting Plasma Glucose, Fasting Serum Insulin (calculate HOMA-IR), HbA1c, Complete Lipid Panel (TC, HDL, LDL, VLDL, Triglycerides).
2. **Reproductive Hormones (Ideally Day 2–4 of natural period):** LH, FSH, Total Testosterone, Free Testosterone (or Calculated FAI), DHEA-S, Prolactin, AMH.
3. **Endocrine Exclusionary Tests:** TSH (rule out hypothyroidism), 17-OH Progesterone (rule out late-onset congenital adrenal hyperplasia).
4. **Micronutrient Baseline:** 25-OH Vitamin D Total, Vitamin B12, Serum Ferritin, CBC Hemoglobin.

### Retesting Intervals:
- **Metabolic Recheck (HbA1c & Fasting Insulin):** Every 3–6 months if insulin resistance or elevated BMI was detected.
- **Vitamin D Retest:** 3 months after starting therapeutic supplementation (target level: 40–60 ng/mL).
- **Post-Trial Endpoint Retest:** Timed to coincide with the conclusion of an active N-of-1 trial phase to objectively measure biomarker shifts.
