Hyperandrogenism in PCOS manifests clinically as hirsutism (excessive terminal hair growth in male-pattern distributions such as chin, chest, upper lip), persistent cystic acne, and androgenetic scalp alopecia.

### Biological Pathways:
The severity of hirsutism correlates with circulating free testosterone and local activity of the enzyme **$5\alpha$-reductase**, which converts testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT) within hair follicles and sebaceous glands.

### Evidence on Spearmint Tea (*Mentha spicata*):
- Two randomized controlled trials demonstrated that drinking 2 cups of organic spearmint tea daily for 30 days significantly reduced free and total testosterone levels in women with PCOS, with statistically significant improvements in subjective Ferriman-Gallwey scores.
- Contraindicated during pregnancy due to historical uterine stimulant concerns.

### Long-term Management:
Hair follicles have anagen growth cycles lasting 6 to 9 months; hence, visible reduction in hair thickness requires at least 3 to 6 months of consistent metabolic and anti-androgenic lifestyle adherence.
