Women diagnosed with PCOS experience a 3-fold higher prevalence of moderate-to-severe anxiety symptoms and a 4-fold higher risk of depressive symptoms compared to age-matched controls.

### Underlying Etiology:
- **Neuroendocrine Dysregulation:** High circulating androgens and altered hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis responsiveness affect mood regulation centers in the amygdala and prefrontal cortex.
- **Biochemical Fluctuations:** Frequent reactive hypoglycemia following high-glycemic meals can mimic or trigger acute panic and palpitations.
- **Psychosocial Stressors:** Concerns regarding fertility, altered physical appearance (hirsutism, hair loss, stubborn weight changes), and feelings of clinical invalidation generate chronic distress.

### Support and Clinical Resources:
- Mental health screening is recommended as a standard component of comprehensive PCOS care.
- Indian National Mental Health Helpline (Tele-MANAS): Dial **14416** or **1800-891-4416** (Toll-free 24/7 support in multiple Indian languages).
- Connecting with qualified psychologists, lifestyle therapists, and structured peer support circles significantly improves health-related quality of life.
