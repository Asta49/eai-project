Traditional modern Indian urban diets frequently derive 65–75% of total caloric intake from high-glycemic carbohydrates (polished white rice, maida, potatoes, sugary chai). For women with PCOS, this pattern triggers pronounced glycemic volatility and elevated postprandial insulin.

### Evidence-Based Dietary Principles:
1. **Optimize Fiber-to-Carbohydrate Ratio:** Target at least 1g of dietary fiber for every 5g of total carbohydrate ($\text{ratio} \ge 0.20$).
2. **Protein-First Meal Sequencing:** Consuming protein and soluble fiber (e.g. dal, paneer, sprouts, salad) 10 minutes prior to starchy carbohydrates slows gastric emptying and reduces glucose peaks by up to 30%.
3. **Smart Culinary Swaps:**
   - White Rice $\to$ Brown Rice, Red Rice, or Foxtail Millet (Kangni)
   - Poha $\to$ Besan Chilla or Sprouted Moong Chilla
   - Sooji Upma $\to$ Broken Wheat Dalia with Vegetables
   - Sweetened Full-Cream Chai $\to$ Unsweetened Cardamom Chai or Spearmint Tea
   - Aloo Samosa $\to$ Roasted Turmeric Makhana or Chana Chaat
4. **Circadian Meal Timing:** Aligning the largest meals with early daytime and eating dinner prior to 8:00 PM improves peripheral insulin sensitivity.
