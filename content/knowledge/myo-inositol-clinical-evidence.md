Inositol is an intracellular second messenger involved in insulin signal transduction and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) signaling in the ovary.

### The 40:1 Physiological Ratio
In physiological conditions, plasma inositol is maintained at a 40:1 ratio between Myo-Inositol (MI) and D-Chiro Inositol (DCI).
- **Myo-Inositol:** Promotes cellular glucose uptake in peripheral tissues and supports FSH signaling in the ovary, promoting follicular maturation and oocyte quality.
- **D-Chiro Inositol:** Promotes glycogen synthesis in the liver. However, excessive DCI concentrations in the ovary impair oocyte quality and aromatase activity.

### Clinical Evidence
Meta-analyses demonstrate that 2,000 to 4,000 mg/day of Myo-Inositol combined with D-Chiro Inositol (at 40:1) significantly:
- Improves spontaneous ovulatory frequency and cycle regularity within 3 months.
- Reduces fasting insulin and HOMA-IR levels comparable to Metformin, with significantly fewer gastrointestinal adverse events.
- Requires physician approval to ensure compatibility with concurrent medications (e.g. Metformin or hormonal contraceptives).
