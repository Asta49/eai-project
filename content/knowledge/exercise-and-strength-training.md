Physical exercise serves as an independent, non-pharmacological insulin sensitizer. Skeletal muscle accounts for approximately 80% of whole-body insulin-mediated glucose disposal.

### Exercise Modalities:
1. **Resistance / Progressive Strength Training (3× per week):**
   - Stimulates translocation of glucose transporter-4 (GLUT4) vesicles to the muscle cell membrane through contraction-activated AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) pathways, bypassing defective insulin receptor phosphorylation.
   - Increases basal metabolic rate (BMR) and reduces visceral adiposity without excessive cortisol production.
2. **Zone 2 Aerobic Walking (30 minutes daily):**
   - Brisk walking post-meals significantly blunts glycemic excursions and enhances lipid oxidation.
   - Low impact, highly sustainable, with zero contraindications.
3. **Recovery and Cortisol Management:**
   - Excessive prolonged high-intensity training without adequate recovery can elevate adrenal cortisol and androgens (DHEA-S). A balanced combination of progressive resistance and restorative walking offers the best clinical outcome.
