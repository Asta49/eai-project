PCOS is the leading cause of anovulatory subfertility globally. However, women with PCOS typically maintain high ovarian reserve (reflected by elevated Anti-Müllerian Hormone / AMH levels), meaning their reproductive potential across their lifespan is preserved once ovulation is restored.

### Clinical Management of Anovulation:
1. **Lifestyle and Weight Optimization:** In women with overweight BMI, a modest 5–10% reduction in body weight restores spontaneous ovulatory cycles in up to 50% of cases by reducing visceral fat and insulin-driven androgen production.
2. **First-Line Pharmacological Induction (Doctor Prescribed):** Letrozole (an aromatase inhibitor) is recommended as the first-line oral ovulation induction agent, demonstrating significantly higher live birth and ovulation rates compared to Clomiphene Citrate.
3. **Preconception Blood Work:**
   - Optimize fasting insulin (HOMA-IR < 2.0).
   - Ensure Serum Vitamin D is > 30 ng/mL (associated with higher implantation rates).
   - Normalize thyroid hormones (TSH < 2.5 mIU/L during preconception).
   - Preconception daily supplementation with 400–800 mcg of active Folate (L-methylfolate).
