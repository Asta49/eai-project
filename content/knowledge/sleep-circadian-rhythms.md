Women with PCOS experience significantly higher rates of sleep architecture disturbances, delayed sleep onset, and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) independent of BMI.

### Endocrine Consequences of Sleep Debt:
- **Cortisol Dysregulation:** Chronic sleep restriction (< 6.5 hours/night) elevates evening cortisol, exacerbating peripheral insulin resistance by up to 25% the following day.
- **Ghrelin and Leptin Alterations:** Sleep deprivation increases the hunger hormone ghrelin while suppressing leptin, driving intense carbohydrate and sugar cravings.
- **Melatonin Secretion:** Ovarian follicular fluid contains high concentrations of melatonin, which functions as an antioxidant shielding developing oocytes from oxidative stress. Low nighttime melatonin impairs follicular development.

### Practical Sleep Hygiene Protocol:
1. Target in-bed time by 10:30–11:00 PM to maximize slow-wave delta sleep.
2. Complete the final meal by 8:00 PM (at least 3 hours prior to sleep) to prevent nocturnal insulin elevation.
3. Dim ambient blue light exposure 60 minutes prior to sleep to support endogenous melatonin synthesis.
